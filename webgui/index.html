Encoding Explanation

Row-major order: Cells are scanned row by row (y=0 to H-1), then column by column (x=0 to W-1).
Nibble packing: Bits are grouped into 4-bit chunks (nibbles). The first bit in the sequence becomes the Most Significant Bit (MSB) of the nibble.
Padding: If total bits aren't a multiple of 4, zeros are padded at the end.
